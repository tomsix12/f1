    Why this exists

    Built for the 6 a.m. qualifying.

    I kept missing sessions. Qualifying at midnight, a sprint at breakfast, lights out while I was stuck at work — and every schedule I found lived in someone else's timezone.

    So this page does one job: it keeps the full season in one place, converts every session to the time you actually live in, and hands you a calendar file with a reminder already attached.

    — Tom, tomsix.xyz

    • 01

      Automatic timezone

      Detected from your browser, switchable in the header. Your choice is remembered locally.

    • 02

      Live schedule

      Session times come from OpenF1 and refresh in the background. A bundled snapshot keeps the page useful if the network doesn't cooperate.

    • 03

      Calendar ready

      Download a single session or a whole weekend as an .ics file. Every event carries a 30-minute heads-up.

    • 04

      No tracking

      No accounts, no analytics, no third-party cookies. Just localStorage for your timezone and a schedule cache.
